How do I get a quote?

Send clear photos of the area, your postcode and the service you need. We assess the size, condition and access before confirming the quote.

Do you cover areas outside Preston?

Yes, work is carried out across Preston and surrounding Lancashire. Send your postcode and we will confirm coverage when we reply.

Do you clean fencing as well as hard surfaces?

Yes. Fence cleaning and suitable fence spraying are part of the exterior-cleaning service range.

Do you offer drain jetting?

Selected domestic external drain-jetting work is available. Send a short description and photos so the job can be assessed for suitability.

Can you handle commercial exterior-cleaning work?

Yes. Commercial enquiries are welcome for suitable premises, yards, forecourts, communal areas and managed sites.
